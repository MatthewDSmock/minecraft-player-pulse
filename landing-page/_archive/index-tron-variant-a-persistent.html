Player Pulse — Minecraft Monetization Sentiment

A rolling sentiment dashboard built from public Minecraft community channels — Reddit, Mojang's official feedback site, Steam (Dungeons + Legends), and the App Store across six English-speaking markets. Pillar-classified by Marketplace, Realms, and Creator on Demand, with every verbatim quote preserved and linked back to its source.

About

Hi, I'm Matthew.

I've spent the last decade in data and analytics — Senior BIE / Data Scientist roles at Microsoft (M365 and Partner Marketing), Target (Web & Apps, rebuilding company-wide attribution), AT&T, and Amazon (Global Media Strategy, including in-game advertising and Twitch). I hold an MBA in Marketing Analytics and a Game Analytics certification from ELVTR.

Right now I'm focused on the spaces where data work directly shapes player experience: monetization that doesn't degrade the product, recommendation systems that surface long-tail creators, and experiment design that respects guardrails.

This site is the public face of my private lab — where I work through ideas in the open, build small things to learn from, and post the writeups that survive.
